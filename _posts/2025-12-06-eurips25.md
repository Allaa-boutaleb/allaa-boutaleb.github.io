**Tusind tak København!** 🇩🇰

I am incredibly honored to have presented our work, **"Exploring Multi-Table Retrieval Through Iterative Search,"** as a **Spotlight Talk** followed by a poster session at the **AI for Tabular Data Workshop** at **EurIPS 2025** in Copenhagen on December 6th.

It was a fantastic experience to engage in insightful discussions with fellow researchers and industry professionals. We dove deep into real-world challenges in multi-table retrieval and how our future work can help solve meaningful problems in this space.

In addition to presenting, I also had the privilege of serving as a **Program Committee (PC) member** for the workshop. Being on the other side of the review process was a rewarding experience that gave me a deeper appreciation for the effort that goes into curating a high-quality technical program.

## About The Work
Multi-table retrieval focuses on gathering and composing information from multiple tables to answer complex natural language questions. Our work introduces an **iterative and explorative strategy** which brings several key benefits:

* 🚀 **Scalability:** It enables efficient heuristics that avoid costly exact optimization. As shown across 5 NL2SQL benchmarks, our method is **4 to 400 times faster** while maintaining competitive performance.
* 👀 **Interpretability:** Each step acts as a visible reasoning checkpoint, making the retrieval process transparent.
* 🧩 **Flexibility:** It opens the door for extensibility and potential human-in-the-loop guidance.
