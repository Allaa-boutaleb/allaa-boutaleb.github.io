And that’s a wrap! 🌟 **BDA 2025** in Toulouse was nothing short of amazing.

I had the pleasure of presenting our recent work and discussing the future of **table retrieval**, specifically highlighting the importance of **interpretability and transparency** in designing tools that help users query large databases with confidence.

It was a fantastic opportunity to meet brilliant researchers in Information Retrieval, NLP, and Knowledge Graphs. The French database community is truly inspiring and welcoming! 🇫🇷

A heartfelt thank you to my supervisors **Bernd Amann**, **Hubert Naacke**, and **Rafael Angarita**, as well as the teams at **IRIT** and **LIP6** for the support.
